# Cardboard Box quest and pack logic verification

**Date:** 2026-10-19. **Baseline:** [SOQM 0.4.9, `6dbd50b03285dc844a5a3bc423ecbe17c31d0145`](https://github.com/lsanarchist/SOQM/commit/6dbd50b03285dc844a5a3bc423ecbe17c31d0145).

## Change boundary

The new optional Mekanism Cardboard Box quest follows Precision Sawmill and
checks possession of one box without consuming it. It has independent quest,
task, reward and caption IDs and an ordinary personal engineering reward.
Ukrainian and English lessons explain the recipe, transport interaction and
the difference between the inventory objective and the suggested chest exercise.

The audit fixes four confirmed problems: the AE2 crafting-CPU duplication path
through Mekanism capture, fusion observation failing when consumers drain the
energy buffer, an incorrect sawdust/PRC lesson and dependency, and missing
reactor observer instructions. See [the audit](../docs/PACK_LOGIC_AUDIT.md) for
player impact, pinned upstream evidence and exact behavior.

The book now has **1,626 quests, 1,773 tasks and 1,627 outer rewards** in 37
chapters, including **1,612 custom rewards**. Every previous quest, task and
reward keeps its ID, ownership and full task/reward definition. The historical
task/reward/repeatability contract remains
`cbc3edbaf2ff9373a3ca8fb2e33488bc589e80e90859ee4e1cbda90934b87be9` after excluding
the explicitly recorded addition. The only removed old dependency is PRC's
unnecessary Precision Sawmill prerequisite.

There are still **122 pinned third-party mods and 12 authored modules**.
Only the authored `qm-quest-automation` binary changes, from 0.4.3 to 0.4.4.
The payload gains the additive `mekanism:cardboard_blacklist` tag and has
**1,836 files**. The 14 reward pools, 326 catalogue entries and 240 distinct
catalogue items remain unchanged.

## Reproduction and limits

```sh
./scripts/check
python3 scripts/render_campaign.py --check
python3 scripts/runtime_probe.py --accept-eula \
  --java /path/to/java21/bin/java \
  --runtime /path/to/installed-neoforge-server \
  --baseline-ref 6dbd50b03285dc844a5a3bc423ecbe17c31d0145 --timeout 600
```

See [the native harness guide](../docs/RUNTIME_VALIDATION.md) for runtime setup.
The native fixture uses a dedicated server, synthetic players, supplied
materials and test power. The box test executes Mekanism's actual capture and
sneak-unpack interactions against live blocks and the loaded data-pack tag.
It checks the CPU capture guard; it does not replay an active autocrafting job
to measure duplicated inputs. The fusion test uses real native reactor data
and the production detector/clock with controlled heat and burning state; it
does not construct and fuel a complete fusion multiblock.

The static recipe audit covers 577 authored definitions and 12,863 native JSON
recipes from 34 pinned JARs. Its abstract reachability model assumes external
resources and tags are available. This is not proof of a complete survival
playthrough. No graphical client, long-term economy, two-client session or
new TPS/FPS performance claim is included.

The previously documented Ex Machinis risk of discarding random output when a
receiver is full remains; its third-party JAR is unchanged. Known nonfatal
upstream warnings also remain. Zero KubeJS/fatal gate errors do not mean a
warning-free console. Raw runtime reports, logs, worlds, rasterizations and
test-only JARs remain ignored local evidence and are excluded from the package.
