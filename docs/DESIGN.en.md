# SOQM Quality Standard

**Shout Out to Questing Mayhem** aims to connect questing, technology, magic and exploration into one coherent adventure. Craftoria is a reference for care, usability and consistent progression. It is not a plan to copy its quests, writing, recipes, artwork or mod count.

The chosen direction is **Expert**: linked recipes and material milestones across mods. This describes the design intent; full campaign balance and completion have not yet been established by playtesting.

- **Readable progression.** Explain goals, prerequisites and next steps. Quest items must be obtainable in the advertised game mode.
- **Purposeful recipes.** Difficulty should introduce decisions and automation. Evaluate recipe changes together with quests, loot and alternative routes.
- **Consistent rewards.** Rewards should help at their stage without silently bypassing later progression. Check their amounts, randomness and exceptions.
- **Usable interface.** Test localization, icons, key bindings, recipe search and error messages in the actual client.
- **Measured technical quality.** Pin dependencies, reproduce imports from a built release and record launches. Performance claims require conditions and measurements.
- **Clear provenance.** Preserve attribution, licenses and sources. Establish redistribution rights before publishing inherited or third-party material.

SOQM begins from the supplied Questing Mayhem Expert 0.4.6 base. Existing `qm` namespaces, `qm_*` filenames and stable quest IDs remain because references, scripts and saved progress depend on them. Display branding can change without renaming internal identifiers.

Historical sources and reports inform the work; current integrations are accepted through their actual behavior and effect on progression. Adding a mod or chapter is not complete without checking that connection.

The first priority is lawful distribution, reproducible import, a verified game start and an accurate alpha status. Full completion, long-term balance, server support and performance become advertised features only after their own verification.
